Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

1. Who we are

LakrisNet Content Planner ("the planner", "we") is a publishing workspace. It lets a user connect their own Pinterest account, choose one of their boards and schedule Pins with their own images. This policy explains what information the planner handles and how.

2. What we collect

  • Workspace account: your name, the email address and the password you use to sign in to the planner itself (the password is stored only as a hash), and your role in the workspace.
  • Content you add: images you upload, Pin titles, descriptions, destination links, the chosen board and the scheduled time. If you use the Discord module, the webhook links of your own channels and the messages you schedule there.
  • Activity records: a log of who created, changed, published or deleted a scheduled Pin and when, so the people in a workspace can see what happened.

We do not collect payment details, contact lists or location data.

3. Data from Pinterest

When you connect Pinterest, you sign in on pinterest.com and approve access there. We never see or store your Pinterest password. Pinterest gives the planner an access token and a refresh token, and with your approval we receive:

  • basic account information: your username and profile picture (user_accounts:read);
  • the list of your boards, so you can choose where to publish (boards:read), and the ability to create a new board when you ask for one (boards:write);
  • the ID, link and status of the Pins the planner publishes for you (pins:read, pins:write).

We only call the Pinterest API to do what you asked: show your account and boards, create a board when you request it, publish the Pins you scheduled and check their status. We do not read other users' content, and we do not follow, save, like or comment on anything for you.

4. How we use it

  • to publish your scheduled Pins at the time you chose;
  • to show you the status of each Pin and a link to it once it is live;
  • to refresh your Pinterest token so scheduled Pins keep working;
  • to keep the planner secure and fix errors.

We do not use your data for advertising or profiling, and we do not sell it.

5. Sharing

The content of a Pin is sent to Pinterest when it is published, because that is what you asked for. Apart from that, we do not share your information with anyone, except if the law requires us to. People you invite to your workspace can see the Pins scheduled in it.

6. Storage and retention

Data is stored on a server we run ourselves, with access limited to the workspace administrators. Pinterest tokens are kept only while your account is connected.

  • When you disconnect Pinterest, its tokens are deleted from the planner right away.
  • Uploaded images, records of published Pins and activity records are kept until you delete them or ask us to.

7. Your choices

  • Disconnect Pinterest at any time inside the planner, or revoke the app's access in your Pinterest account settings.
  • Delete any scheduled Pin or uploaded image yourself.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data or to delete all of it by writing to the address below. We reply within 30 days.

Pins already published stay on Pinterest until you remove them there.

8. Cookies

The planner uses one session cookie to keep you signed in. It does not use advertising or tracking cookies. This public website does not set cookies; it only remembers in your browser's local storage whether you are signed in to the planner, so it can show your initial instead of "Sign in". Its fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If a change affects how Pinterest data is used, we will tell connected users before it takes effect.

10. Contact
