Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how LakrisNet Content Planner handles information used to provide a content planning and publishing workspace for creators and small teams.

Last updated: March 2026

1. What the product does

LakrisNet Content Planner is presented as a workflow tool for planning, reviewing, scheduling, and monitoring creator-owned or otherwise authorized content across connected platforms.

2. Information we use

We may process account connection data, scheduling metadata, internal audit records, and user- submitted content details needed to operate queue, review, and publishing workflows.

3. Connected accounts

Where a user connects a third-party account, the site is intended to use OAuth-based authorization. We do not ask users to provide platform passwords directly through this public-facing website.

4. How data is used

Information is used to provide scheduling, queue management, review visibility, reporting, and related product functions for the account holder or authorized team members.

5. Retention and security

We aim to retain only the minimum data necessary to provide workflow functionality and to protect account information through reasonable administrative and technical safeguards.
