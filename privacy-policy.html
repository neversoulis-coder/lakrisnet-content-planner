LakrisNet Content Planner

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how LakrisNet Content Planner collects, uses, and protects information when users access our content planning and publishing workspace.

1. Information we collect

We may collect account information provided by the user, OAuth-based authorization data, workspace configuration details, scheduling metadata, publishing history, audit events, and support communication records.

2. How we use information

We use collected information to authenticate connected accounts, support content planning, queue review, scheduled publishing workflows, internal audit visibility, duplicate-prevention checks, and first-party reporting for the authenticated user’s own content and account.

3. OAuth and connected accounts

We do not ask users to provide third-party account passwords directly to us when OAuth-based authentication is available. Access is limited to the permissions granted by the user.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. We do not combine one user’s connected account data with another user’s account for resale, off-platform advertising, or competitor intelligence. We may share limited information with service providers that help us host or operate the service, subject to confidentiality and security obligations.

5. Data retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, support lawful business operations, resolve disputes, and comply with legal obligations.

6. User rights and requests

Users may contact us regarding access, correction, deletion, or questions about their data, subject to applicable law and our operational requirements.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect data, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Children

Our service is not directed to children under the age required by applicable law to use the relevant online services independently.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will be posted on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact: support@lakrisnet.example
