Features

A calm queue, not a bulk-posting machine.

Clear status
Every Pin is scheduled, published (with a link to the live Pin) or failed with the reason Pinterest gave, so you can fix it and try again.
Edit or cancel
Until a Pin is published you can change its text, board, image or time, or take it out of the queue.
Duplicate check
You get a warning if the same image is already in the queue or was published recently.
Stays connected
Tokens are refreshed the way Pinterest describes, so a scheduled Pin doesn't fail because a token expired overnight.
Other platforms
The same workspace can post to your own Discord channels through webhooks. Reddit and DeviantArt modules are in progress.
Data & permissions

What we ask Pinterest for, and why.

user_accounts:read

Show which Pinterest account is connected (username and profile picture).

boards:read

List your boards so you can choose where a Pin is published.

boards:write

Create a new board, only when you ask for one in the planner.

pins:read

Check the status and link of Pins the planner published for you.

pins:write

Publish the Pins you scheduled, at the time you chose.

What we never do

  • Ask for or store your Pinterest password.
  • Follow, like, comment or save on your behalf.
  • Read or collect other people's Pins and boards.
  • Sell, share or use your data for advertising.

Disconnect inside the planner or revoke access in your Pinterest settings at any time. More in the privacy policy.
