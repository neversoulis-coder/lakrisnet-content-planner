Suggested site copy

Public product summary

LakrisNet Content Planner is a publishing workspace for creators and small teams who manage original content across connected platforms.

The Pinterest module lets an authenticated user connect their own account, choose boards, prepare visual content, schedule individual Pins, and review posting status and first-party performance information for that same account.

The product is presented as a controlled workflow tool with optional review, audit visibility, and duplicate-prevention checks. It is not framed as a bulk automation or engagement manipulation service.
